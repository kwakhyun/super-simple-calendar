# Simple Calendar Account Deletion

Simple Calendar lets users delete their account in the app.

## In-App Deletion

1. Open Simple Calendar.
2. Open Settings.
3. In Account, choose Delete account.
4. Confirm deletion.

This permanently deletes the server account, linked social login records, verification codes, and account token records. Memos stored only on the device remain on that device.

## Deletion Request Without the App

If you no longer have access to the app, email khyun9685@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Simple Calendar account and request account deletion.

We use the email address to verify account ownership before processing the request.
