# Simple Calendar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-24

Simple Calendar stores calendar memos on your device. If you create an account, the app collects your email address and authentication information so you can sign in and verify your account.

## Data We Collect

- Email address, when you sign up or sign in.
- Authentication identifiers from Apple, Google, or Kakao if you choose social login.
- Verification codes and login tokens needed to secure your account.
- Technical request data such as IP address may be processed by the API host and email provider for security and delivery.

## Data We Do Not Collect

- Calendar memos are stored locally on your device in the current app version.
- We do not sell personal data.
- We do not use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.

## Service Providers

Simple Calendar uses Fly.io for API hosting and Resend for account verification email delivery.

## Account Deletion

You can delete your account in the app from Settings > Account > Delete account. Account deletion removes your server account, OAuth links, email verification records, and revoked login token records associated with the account. Memos saved only on your device remain on that device unless you delete the app data.

If you cannot access the app, email khyun9685@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account and request account deletion for Simple Calendar.
